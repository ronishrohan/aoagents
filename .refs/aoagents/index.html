How it works

From a one-line task to a merged PR.

Everything below is the actual AO desktop app, doing real work.

Delegate

Tell the orchestrator what you need.

Every project gets a main agent that runs the fleet for you. Describe the outcome — it plans the work, spawns workers into their own worktrees, keeps them moving, and escalates only what needs a human.

plans + spawns workerskeeps sessions movingyou stay the reviewer
Watch

The whole fleet, at a glance.

Every session flows across one board — working, needs you, in review, ready to merge — with its agent, branch, and PR state on the card. You see what needs attention and ignore the rest.

live session statusPR + CI state per cardmulti-project
Close the loop

CI fails. The right agent hears about it.

AO watches every PR it opens. Failed checks and review comments route back to the session that owns the branch — with the context to fix them — until the PR is approved.

CI + review routingfull activity timelineone click to the PR
Quickstart

Zero to a fleet in three steps.

No account, no cloud, no credit card. AO runs as a local daemon on your machine — your code never leaves localhost.

01~1 min

Install the desktop app

One cask on macOS, or grab the installer for your platform. The app runs and updates the daemon for you.

macOS (Apple silicon)macOS (Intel)WindowsLinux
02~2 min

Sign in to GitHub

One command — AO reads issues, PRs, reviews, and CI as you. The agent CLI you already use just works. That's the whole setup.

03ongoing

Tell the orchestrator what you need

Just say it in plain words. The orchestrator plans the work, spawns workers into their own worktrees, and you watch the PRs land on the board.

You → “Go through my Linear backlog and let’s brainstorm what tasks to spawn.”
